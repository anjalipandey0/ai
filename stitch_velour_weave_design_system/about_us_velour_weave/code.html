Velour & Weave represents the pinnacle of sustainable luxury. We honor the slow, meticulous art of traditional textile creation, bringing heritage techniques into modern, high-end interiors.

The Process

From Soil to Silhouette

01

Cultivation

Partnering exclusively with multi-generational organic farms, we source long-staple fibers cultivated without synthetic interventions. Every harvest respects the soil's natural restorative cycle.

02

Spinning

Raw fibers are hand-spun using heritage techniques that preserve natural variations in thickness, imparting a unique, organic tactile quality to every spool of thread.
